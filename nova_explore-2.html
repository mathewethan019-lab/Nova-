Public access first

NOVA lists public-facing destinations, not instructions to enter closed buildings or restricted land. Confirm current access before travelling.

Map tiles and map data are provided by OpenStreetMap contributors. This demo has a curated starter list, not a complete worldwide database. Opening times, closures, ticketing and access rules can change; follow signs and official local guidance. Remote or restricted areas may not be safe or legal to enter.
